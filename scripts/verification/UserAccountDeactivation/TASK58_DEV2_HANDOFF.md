Actor/history reads may need **locking** for transaction compatibility without becoming new
current-work predicates or imposing future-owner eligibility on historical users. A retained
User U lock before features also excludes Deactivate's Users TABLOCKX during that transaction;
therefore Assign/Return/Create/Accept/MR paths with such locks must not be accused of the same
unprotected feature-first race merely because an ordinary actor read appears later. Full
multi-writer graph/isolation evidence remains necessary; a potential edge is not a proven deadlock.
