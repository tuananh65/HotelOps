3.3 Data Business Rules
[Rules about data that are not obvious from the ERD. ]
Rule
Description
Enforced On
Unique username
No two users may share the same username, regardless of role.
Account creation and username change
Unique email
No two users may share the same email address.
Account creation and email change
One application per job
A Candidate may not submit a second Application for a job they have already applied to, even if the previous was withdrawn.
Application submission
Application requires active job
A Candidate may only apply for a Job Posting with status Active.
Application submission
Accounts never permanently deleted
User accounts are deactivated, not deleted. All associated data must remain accessible.
Account deactivation
Jobs with applications cannot be deleted
A Job Posting with at least one Application (any status) cannot be deleted — must be Closed instead.
Job deletion
Internal notes are permanent
Once saved, an Internal Note cannot be edited or deleted by anyone. They form an audit trail.
Note creation
Evaluations are permanent
Once an Interviewer submits rating and feedback, neither can be changed by anyone.
Evaluation submission
CV files are access-controlled
CV files must never be accessible via a guessable or publicly shareable link. Access verified by system on every download.
CV file access
4. User Requirements
4.1 Actors
#
Actor
Description
1
Administrator
Quản trị viên hệ thống. Chịu trách nhiệm thiết lập dữ liệu gốc của khách sạn (hotel master data), quản lý tài khoản/quyền truy cập, và thiết lập cấu hình kho. Có quyền tra cứu và nhận các báo cáo nhật ký thay đổi của hệ thống (audit logs).
2
Operations Manager
Quản lý vận hành. Xử lý các dữ liệu quản lý phòng, quản lý tài sản, điều chỉnh hoặc hủy giao dịch kho, và giải quyết các sự cố leo thang. Thiết lập các tiêu chí giám sát, yêu cầu báo cáo và tiếp nhận các dashboard/báo cáo về tình trạng sẵn sàng của toàn hệ thống.
3
Housekeeping Supervisor
Giám sát buồng phòng. Phụ trách quản lý, phân công công việc vệ sinh, và thiết lập dữ liệu/checklist kiểm tra phòng. Tra cứu lịch sử luân chuyển phòng, nhận các cảnh báo liên quan đến vệ sinh/kiểm tra và theo dõi tình trạng sẵn sàng của phòng.
4
Housekeeping Staff
Nhân viên dọn phòng. Nhận các thông báo phân công công việc và thông tin lỗi sau khi giám sát kiểm tra. Gửi lại hệ thống dữ liệu thực hiện công việc vệ sinh và kết quả dọn lại (rework) nếu có.
5
Maintenance Lead
Trưởng nhóm kỹ thuật/bảo trì. Chịu trách nhiệm quản lý dữ liệu sự cố, thiết lập lịch bảo trì phòng ngừa định kỳ và tra cứu lịch sử bảo trì tài sản. Nhận các cảnh báo khi có sự cố nghiêm trọng (critical incident) xảy ra.
6
Maintenance Staff
Nhân viên kỹ thuật/bảo trì. Nhận thông báo công việc, thông tin phân công sửa chữa và lịch bảo trì định kỳ từ hệ thống. Trực tiếp thực hiện và cập nhật dữ liệu tiến độ/kết quả sửa chữa.
7
Storekeeper
Thủ kho. Quản lý dữ liệu gốc vật tư, ghi nhận các giao dịch xuất/nhập/tồn kho, cấp phát vật tư thực tế và tạo yêu cầu bổ sung kho. Nhận các cảnh báo tồn kho thấp, danh sách yêu cầu vật tư đã được duyệt và thông tin biến động số dư kho.
8
Department Head
Trưởng bộ phận. Quản lý các công việc vận hành, phê duyệt yêu cầu cấp phát vật tư, quản lý yêu cầu nội bộ, bàn giao tài sản và tra cứu khối lượng công việc của nhân sự. Nhận thông báo chờ phê duyệt, cảnh báo công việc quá hạn và thông tin chi tiết về khối lượng công việc.
9
Owner
Chủ sở hữu/Ban giám đốc. Yêu cầu hệ thống trích xuất các dashboard và báo cáo. Nhận và theo dõi các dashboard vận hành, báo cáo phân tích tổng hợp cùng các thông báo quản lý cấp cao.

1. Authentication & Role Management (FE-01)
1.1 UC-001 Login
Primary Actors
Administrator, Operations Manager, Owner, Housekeeping Supervisor, Housekeeping Staff, Maintenance Lead, Maintenance Staff, Storekeeper, Department Head
Secondary Actors
None
Description
Allows an internal user to sign in with a provisioned account so that the system can identify the user and apply the user's roles and scope.
Preconditions
The user has an internal account created by the Administrator (UC-004).
Normal Flow
Login
User opens the Login page.
System displays the Login form (username or email, password) and the Forgot Password link.
User enters the username or email and the password and selects Login.
System checks that both fields are filled (GBR-003).
System finds the account and verifies the password (GBR-019).
System checks that the account is Active and not locked (GBR-020).
System resets the failed-attempt counter, creates a session and calculates the effective permissions from the user's roles and scope (GBR-021, GBR-002).
System records the login in the audit log (GBR-008).
System opens the user's home page (notifications and the functions allowed for the user's roles).
Alternative Flows
Step 2.1_Forgot Password
User selects Forgot Password and enters a username or email.
System displays the same instruction for every input, without revealing whether the account exists (MSG14, GBR-024).
The flow ends.
Step 4.1_Missing Information
Username/email or password is empty.
System shows which field is required (MSG03).
Return to step 3 of the normal flow.
Step 5.1_Invalid Credentials
The account does not exist, the password is wrong, or the account is Inactive.
If the account exists, System increases its failed-attempt counter (GBR-020).
System displays the same generic message for all three cases (MSG11, GBR-019).
Return to step 3 of the normal flow.
Step 5.2_Lock Threshold Reached
The failed attempt reaches SYS-PAR-01.
System sets locked_until = now + SYS-PAR-02 minutes (GBR-020).
System records the lock in the audit log (GBR-008).
System displays MSG11; the flow ends.
Step 6.1_Account Temporarily Locked
The password is correct but locked_until is later than now.
System does not create a session and displays the unlock time (MSG12, GBR-020).
The flow ends.
Step 7.1_Temporary Password
The password used is a valid temporary password.
System creates a restricted session that only allows changing the password (GBR-023).
System opens the Change Password screen (UC-003); the flow ends.
Step 7.2_Temporary Password Expired
The temporary password is older than SYS-PAR-05 hours.
System does not create a session (MSG13, GBR-023).
The flow ends.
Postconditions
Success: an active session exists and the user sees only functions granted by the user's roles and scope.
Failure: no session is created; failed attempts and locks are recorded.

1.3 UC-003 Change Password
Primary Actors
Administrator, Operations Manager, Owner, Housekeeping Supervisor, Housekeeping Staff, Maintenance Lead, Maintenance Staff, Storekeeper, Department Head
Secondary Actors
None
Description
Allows a user to change their own password, including the mandatory change after receiving a temporary password.
Preconditions
User has logged in and has permission for this function (GBR-001, GBR-002).
For a forced change, the user is in a restricted session created from a temporary password (UC-001, Step 7.1).
Normal Flow
Change Password
User opens Change Password.
System displays fields: current password, new password, confirm new password, and the password policy.
User enters the three fields and selects Save.
System verifies the current password (GBR-019).
System checks that the new password matches the confirmation and satisfies the password policy (GBR-022).
System stores the new password hash, clears the temporary-password flag and revokes all other sessions of the user (GBR-021, GBR-023).
System records the change in the audit log without the password values (GBR-008).
System displays a success message (MSG08).
Alternative Flows
Step 4.1_Wrong Current Password
The current password is incorrect.
System displays MSG03 for the current password field.
Return to step 3 of the normal flow.
Step 5.1_Policy Not Met
The new password does not match the confirmation, violates the policy, or equals the current password.
System lists the unmet policy items (MSG03, GBR-022).
Return to step 3 of the normal flow.
Postconditions
The new password is effective; other sessions are revoked; the restricted session (if any) becomes a normal session.

User has logged in and has permission for this function (GBR-001, GBR-002).
The incident is Pending Triage and has category and severity (UC-050).
Normal Flow
Triage Incident
Maintenance Lead opens the incident and selects Triage.
System displays priority options and calculates default response and resolution deadlines from the detected time and the SLA targets of each priority (GBR-069).
Maintenance Lead selects the priority, reviews the deadlines and enters the initial action plan, then selects Save.
System validates the input and business conditions (GBR-069, GBR-014, GBR-005).
System changes the incident from Pending Triage to Pending Assignment.
System saves the triage and records the audit log in the same transaction (GBR-007, GBR-008).
System opens Assign Maintenance Work (UC-053).
Alternative Flows
Step 3.1_Deadline Changed
Maintenance Lead changes a default deadline.
System requires a reason and stores the default and chosen deadline (GBR-069, GBR-012).
Return to step 4 of the normal flow.
Step 3.2_Duplicate or Invalid Incident
Maintenance Lead selects Cancel as Duplicate/Invalid.
System requires the original incident (for Duplicate) and a reason (GBR-070).
System asks User to confirm the cancellation of the incident (MSG33).
System changes the incident to Cancelled and removes its effective blocks, recalculating room status (GBR-075, GBR-043).
System saves the cancellation and records the audit log in the same transaction (GBR-007, GBR-008).
System sends an in-app notification to the reporter (GBR-010).
The flow ends.
Step 4.1_Not Classified
Category or severity is missing.
System displays MSG03 and opens UC-050.
The flow ends.
Postconditions
The incident has a priority and SLA deadlines and waits for assignment.

5. Background Job Inventory
5.1 Unlock Expired Locked Accounts (JOB-01)
Trigger: Scheduled — every 15 minutes
Purpose: Automatically unlocks accounts that were locked after failed sign-in attempts, once the lockout duration has elapsed
Processing steps:
Find all accounts with status = Locked where the lockout timestamp is more than 10 minutes ago.
For each account found: reset the failed sign-in count to zero, set status back to Active.
Log the number of accounts unlocked in this run.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
System behavior
Database unreachable
Job retries up to 3 times with 30-second intervals. If still failing, logs a critical error. Accounts remain locked until the next successful run.
No locked accounts found
Job completes immediately with no action — this is normal and expected.
5.2 Clean Up Old Audit Log Entries (JOB-02)
Trigger: Scheduled — daily at 2:00 AM
Purpose: Removes audit log entries older than the configured retention period to prevent unbounded database growth.
Processing steps:
Determine the configured log retention period (default: 90 days).
Find all audit log entries with a timestamp older than the retention threshold.
Delete the old entries in batches of up to 5,000 records per run — to avoid locking the database for an extended period.
If records remain after one batch, schedule the next batch to run 5 minutes later rather than deleting everything at once.
Log the total number of entries deleted and the number of batches processed.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
System behavior
Database timeout during delete
Current batch rolled back. Job logs a warning. Tries again at the next scheduled time. No data corruption.
Retention period not configured
Job uses default of 90 days and logs a warning that the setting was not found.
